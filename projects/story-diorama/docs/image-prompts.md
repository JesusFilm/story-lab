## building

Primary request: Noah, elderly bearded man in ochre linen, stands beneath the immense ribs of an unfinished wooden ark on dry land. Small distant onlookers gesture skeptically. Slanting late golden sunlight, sawdust and cloud shadows; a solemn calling and human doubt.

## animals

Primary request: Pairs of elephants, sheep, deer and birds approach the open ramp of the completed massive rectangular timber ark. Noah in ochre linen stands beside the doorway, tiny against its scale. Quiet ordered procession, luminous silver and warm gold sky before rain.

## flood

Primary request: The same enormous enclosed timber ark with a long rectangular hull and low pitched roof, door sealed, floats alone on a boundless rising dark sea in sustained rainfall. Mountains almost submerged in the far distance. Awe and grief conveyed by immense water, no bodies or graphic injury, no lightning spectacle.
