# Shepherd Maze

- Status: preserved original alternative; runnable for comparison
- Owner: Jaco (direction); Codex (prototype restoration)
- Last updated: 2026-09-11

## Premise and challenge

A shepherd navigates a maze to find baby Jesus in a stall. The challenge is choosing
routes through a confusing layout, remembering recent movement and recovering from
dead ends. The biblical frame is the shepherds' announcement and arrival in Luke 2:8–20.
The maze, routes and control rules are creative game design.

The [restored prototype](../../prototypes/shepherd-maze/README.md) uses automatic
walk/run movement, contextual arrow choices at junctions, fading footprints and
Easy/Medium/Maximum navigation assistance. It defaults to the Blender character
and village, with a live V1/V2 switch to the generated Tripo presentation.

The original concept proposed losing lives when stuck and encountering Scripture
along the way. These are historical ideas, not implemented or settled requirements.
The current prototype offers forgiving navigation help and records completion type.

## Relationship to Shepherd Adventure

Jaco pivoted toward exploration and discovery after finding the maze experience
unsatisfying. [Shepherd Adventure](../shepherd-adventure/concept.md) is the current
direction and has its own concept and prototype. Both share the Nativity story and
[asset catalog](asset-catalog.md). Preserve this maze for demonstrations and comparison;
do not fold further adventure mechanics into it.

The prototype was restored from the pre-adventure source snapshot dated 10 September,
with relocation fixes and Blender selected by default. Its editable world, maps,
source archives and generated alternatives remain available. The Git baseline retains
the former combined concept document and prototype layout.
