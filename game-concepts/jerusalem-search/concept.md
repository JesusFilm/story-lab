# Jerusalem search — Where is Jesus?

**Status: DRAFT — idea phase.** Last updated: 8 September 2026.
This is a potential second Watch Game experience. Its game type and mechanics have
not been determined, and it is not committed for implementation.

## Story premise

Mary and Joseph realize Jesus is missing from their traveling group. They return to
Jerusalem, in Israel, to find him. The existing narrative reference follows the
finding of Jesus among the teachers in the Temple (Luke 2:41–52). This story concerns
Jesus as a boy, distinct from the baby Jesus destination of the shepherd maze.

The premise offers a possible experience about returning, searching and reunion.
How the player participates remains open.

## Existing exploration

[Storyline 2 in reference pack v1](../../assets/references/reference-pack-v1/storyline-2-temple/README.md)

## Questions to resolve before implementation

- What game type and core interaction best serve the story?
- Who does the player control, and what choices are meaningful?
- What audience, device, session length and accessibility needs should guide design?
- What is the smallest experiment that could test the proposed interaction?
- Should this idea be pursued, and who will own the next proposal?

Keep new proposals and game-specific plans in this directory with explicit status
and ownership. Record an accepted design decision before describing any proposed
mechanic as settled.
