Audio credits

Recordings adapted with mono conversion, filtering, level normalization, fades and MP3 encoding. Frog recording trimmed to three seconds. Looping beds receive a two-second equal-power overlap after decoding. These are atmospheric interpretations, not a verified reconstruction of ancient Judean wildlife or speech.

  • crickets.mp3: Crickets — FreethinkerAnon, CC0 1.0.
  • sheep.mp3: Sheep #7 — Joseph SARDIN / BigSoundBank, CC0.
  • frog.mp3: Green frog, croaking.wav — SpliceSound, CC0 1.0.
  • house-murmur.mp3: Muffled Talking — PuplPndaHimwx, CC0 1.0. Low-pass filtered to obscure speech.
  • jackal.mp3: Jackals howling in the mountains — Babadeda, CC0 1.0. Short 18.3–22.6 second excerpt selected, filtered and faded; the full chorus is not used.
  • wolf.mp3: Lone wolf howling — mokasza, CC BY 4.0. The author identifies this as an AI-generated sound made with ElevenLabs, not a wildlife recording. Filtered, normalized and faded for distant playback.
  • house-woman.mp3: Muffled talking (female) — UnrealQW, CC0 1.0. Single female speech behind a wall; filtered and normalized 3 dB below the original house murmur, with the same runtime loop crossfade.
