# House 1 rehearsal sound

`resident-refusal.mp3` is temporary synthesized dialogue: “Go away! It is late!”
Created locally with the installed macOS Daniel voice at 185 words/minute,
then filtered to 180–1800 Hz with FFmpeg to suggest a closed door. Duration:
approximately 1.6 seconds. No real person's recording or identity is represented.

The three wooden knocks are generated at runtime with short noise transients and
damped resonances in `src/house-scene.mjs`. Subtitles retain the scene's meaning
when audio is unavailable. Voice performance remains subject to user review;
this is rehearsal material, not an approved final performance or published asset.
