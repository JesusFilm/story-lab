## Direction and current draft

The user selected a guided sequence, one part at a time, with no wrong combinations.
The draft opens a close view beside a linen-colored item panel:

1. **Lamp body** — “A small lamp to carry through the village.” → **Use this lamp**
2. **Linen wick** — “Fit the wick into the little oil cup.” → **Fit wick**
3. **Oil** — “Pour a little oil into the lamp.” → **Add oil**
4. **Flint & tinder** — “Take what you need to kindle a flame.” → **Take flint**
5. **A light for the road** — “Everything is ready. Bring the little flame to life.” → **Light lamp**

Each item has a large PNG: lamp and jar are renders of the actual models; wick and
flint are native ImageGen illustrations. Short feedback confirms the preceding
action. Wick/flint disappear from the table when used. The jar remains.

The table lamp glows after the final action. **Take lamp** removes it from the
bench, attaches the carried light to the right hand and shows **+1 Lamp** for four
active seconds. **Set out — House 1** walks the existing next corridor. Preparation
and collection are untimed; the reward display never blocks departure.

Back to workbench retains the current step. Pause blocks actions and pauses reward
time. Jump/replay of point 01 and restart reset preparation; later review jumps
reconstruct the completed lamp. Space cannot bypass unfinished preparation.
