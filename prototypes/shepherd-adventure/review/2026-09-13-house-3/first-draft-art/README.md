# House 3 illustrated exchange

Three original images made with the built-in ImageGen tool for this prototype's
brief, player-paced resident exchange. These are illustrations of the scene;
the runtime 3D door stays closed. The resident and sighting are authored village
fiction, not a scripture quotation. No voice track accompanies this exchange.

- `lit-door.png`: lit window and closed door.
- `opening-door.png`: door opening and resident emerging.
- `helpful-resident.png`: resident speaking in the doorway.

The opening and speaking frames use the preceding frame as their edit reference.
The runtime uses these local copies. No external image service is needed to play.

## Generation prompts

### Lit door

Use case: illustration-story. Create a single landscape 3:2 storybook image for a biblical village game at night. Stylized handcrafted 3D clay-like illustration, softly rounded cream limestone blocks, timber lintel, simple flat-roof house. Close view of the front facade: closed wooden plank door with dark iron ring handle slightly right of center, one small square warmly amber-lit window to the left. Blue moonlit stone and warm welcoming light, quiet gentle atmosphere, simple earthen lane. Eye level, house fills frame, door fully visible. No people, no text, no UI, no border or watermark. This is first in a consistent sequence; composition must allow the door to open inward later and a friendly middle-aged male resident to stand in the doorway.

### Door opening

Edit this story illustration into the next frame. Keep the exact camera, house facade, window, props, moon, colors and stylized clay-render medium. Change only the wooden door: now partly open inward on its left hinge, warm amber light spills from the interior across the threshold. A friendly middle-aged male resident with olive skin, short dark curly hair and short beard wearing a simple ochre tunic is just visible inside, hand gently opening the door, looking kindly toward the unseen visitor/camera. Gentle, understated, natural proportions. No text or UI. Maintain landscape 3:2.

### Helpful resident

Edit into the third frame of this illustrated exchange. Keep the exact camera, house, props, moon, colors, clay-like rendered style and SAME friendly resident identity, hair, face, beard, ochre tunic. Door now open wider inward, resident standing clearly visible at threshold speaking gently to unseen visitor. Preserve all surrounding composition.
