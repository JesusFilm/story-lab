## Specific play-test

Knock once and watch the free hand and three door cues. The window lights after
2.5 seconds; the resident speaks at 3.3 seconds. The window goes dark at 5.9 seconds,
and the next action appears at 6.7 seconds, after the shepherd steps back.
Choose **Try the lit house — House 3** and follow the unchanged western lane.
Review the irritation of the temporary voice, pause before the reply, text,
light strength, and whether the next action feels natural. Try Pause during the
reply and Replay approach afterward. The user play-tested this sequence successfully and accepted it on 13 September 2026.
