About this telling

Scripture is from the Berean Standard Bible (public domain). The skeptical onlookers are an artistic dramatization; Genesis does not explicitly describe people ridiculing Noah. The closing painting combines the dove and rainbow as an illustration of renewal.

The flood passage names the loss of life. The paintings contain no graphic imagery.
