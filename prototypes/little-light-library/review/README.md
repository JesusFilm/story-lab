Goal: match the tactile, animated illustrated-book experience demonstrated in the [creator's X post](https://x.com/MengTo/status/2098027027760840864) and [StoryComet](https://storycomet.app/). Functional coverage is not visual quality. Keep the complete two-book/nine-locale scope.

Every round: (1) play reader and shelf figurines visibly, (2) critique, (3) revisit reference, (4) implement a coherent pass, (5) compare matched captures and score each criterion. Retain gains, revert regressions. Scores are implementing-agent judgment, not user approval. Do not average away weak reading, motion or sound. Sound remains unassessed without listening. Screenshots cannot prove smooth motion; retain sequences/recordings and runtime motion evidence separately.

Rubric, fixed before edits: 1 absent/broken, 2 rudimentary, 3 coherent but visibly limited, 4 polished with minor shortcomings, 5 reference-level judged from direct play. Criteria: room atmosphere/spatial grounding; physical book choreography; pop-up depth/character acting; reading composition/responsiveness; tactile interaction; sound atmosphere. Completion requires direct evidence for every criterion and no remaining serious regressions.
