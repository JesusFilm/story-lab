# Eden paper-theatre art

This folder catalogs original art and source prompts for the eight-page Eden book. Read-aloud text, all nine localized editions, narration, full-spread illustrations and generated book index remain separate:

- Story text and localized titles: [public/content](../../../public/content/)
- Existing full-spread illustrations: [public/assets/art/eden-01.webp–eden-08.webp](../../../public/assets/art/)
- Eden/Noah staging types and directions: [src/eden-stage-direction.ts](../../../src/eden-stage-direction.ts) and [src/stage-direction.ts](../../../src/stage-direction.ts)
- Per-page passages, text, narration cues and scene notes: [docs/books/eden.md](../../../docs/books/eden.md)
- Localized measured narration: [public/audio-manifest.json](../../../public/audio-manifest.json) and [public/assets/audio](../../../public/assets/audio/)

Each active cutout has an editable transparent PNG under `source-art/`, a readable generation prompt under `prompts/`, and a runtime WebP under `public/assets/art/theatre/`. Runtime cutouts use WebP quality 84 and preserve alpha. The character art uses the `ActorDirection.image` stage contract, so each adult remains individually selectable and named while the artwork stays rigid and fully modest. Image-mode widths are set from each runtime image’s painted alpha bounds; pages 1–3 use tall, opaque bushes that hide the entire body below the shoulders while preserving a consistent 1.95-unit card height.

## Page staging map

| Page | Backcloth and ground                        | Character/art action                                                                                                                                       |
| ---- | ------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1    | Garden + continuous garden ground           | Adam stands behind tall opaque foliage; only his head, shoulders, arms and hands rise above it.                                                            |
| 2    | Garden + continuous garden ground           | Eve joins Adam behind separate full-height bush cutouts; both remain concealed below the shoulders, and Eve faces Adam.                                    |
| 3    | Garden + continuous garden ground           | Eve holds the fruit toward Adam, who reaches to receive it; tall bushes conceal both bodies, and the serpent keeps its separate interactive character rig. |
| 4    | Garden + continuous garden ground           | Separate leaf-garment shame poses show Adam and Eve hiding after their realization.                                                                        |
| 5    | Garden + continuous garden ground           | A distinct, subdued listening pose keeps the leaf garments while the consequences are told.                                                                |
| 6    | Natural guarded-way backcloth + exile earth | Animal-hide garments and walking poses show the couple leaving the garden; firelight is symbolic and no God or angel is pictured.                          |
| 7    | Exile + exile earth                         | Work poses, a seed basket, and gathered grain distinguish hard work from the departure scene.                                                              |
| 8    | Exile + exile earth                         | New animal-hide poses show a quiet, forward-looking hope without implying that life outside Eden is easy.                                                  |

The older [fig-leaf hiding screen](source-art/fig-leaf-hiding-screen.png) and [fieldwork tool group](source-art/fieldwork-tools.png) are retained source studies only; the active character cutouts now carry the page-4 leaf clothing and page-7 work actions. Shared `garden.webp`, `exile.webp`, `eden-tree.webp`, actor-pose atlases and `serpent-branch.webp` remain separate legacy/runtime scenery and creature art. The eight full-page Eden illustrations are not substitutes for the layered stage backcloth, ground print and cutout contract.
