# Independent quality review of round 20

Historical review: raw iteration captures and recordings were deleted during repository cleanup. The observations below record checks performed at the time; removed evidence is not available for reinspection. Current verification is in [review/latest](../review/latest/).

Reviewed all 48 matched settled views at 1366×768, 768×1024, and 360×800 in `before/` and final `candidate-02/`, with close comparison of changed Eden 01–05 and representative unchanged Eden/Noah spreads. I also inspected all 40 desktop/phone folding poses in `folding-final/` and five normal, missing, delayed, superseded, and reduced-motion states in `lifecycle-02/`. I did not inspect the implementation or launch a browser. Candidate 01 was rejected as visually ineffective: its floor sat beneath the paper and the available tablet Eden frames were pixel-identical to baseline. These stills establish appearance at sampled states, not perceived continuous motion, performance, or sound.

**Retain candidate 02.** The baseline desktop Eden 01 leaves Adam and the tree standing over a broad empty cream leaf. The candidate desktop Eden 01 places them on a quiet painted grass-and-earth wash. The pale center supports Adam's silhouette and shadow, while denser edge foliage visually connects the freestanding tree and rear garden painting to the horizontal page. It reads as a stage ground rather than a second backdrop.

The construction remains recognizably paper. Cream margins surround the irregular painted edge; the center gutter and ribbon cross cleanly above it; the fore edge and woven page thickness remain visible. Actor, tree, and serpent shadows land on the wash without becoming muddy. Desktop Eden 03 keeps Eve, fruit, serpent, and Adam distinct against the restrained center, while tablet Eden 02 shows the same grounding without competing with the text card. At phone size, Eden 01 and Eden 03 retain clear silhouettes, book edges, controls, and readable text.

The improvement is consistent through Eden 05, but the repeated ground creates the main narrative tradeoff. Eden 04 and Eden 05 still use the same green-edged garden patch after shame and during the consequences. Their bowed poses and text preserve the moral shift, and the floor's worn tan center is restrained enough that it does not make the scenes celebratory. Even so, a slightly drier or less saturated variant for 04–05 would express the change more deliberately and reduce the sense of one reusable stage carpet. Eden 06 removes the floor as the pair leaves the garden; the barren backdrop and cream page remain unchanged, so the broader garden-to-exile transition is intact.

All sampled unchanged views remain compositionally stable. Eden 06–08 retain their prior page treatment, and representative Noah family, flood, ark, and rainbow views match the baseline without an accidental garden layer. Text, highlights, navigation, playback controls, critical figures, and props remain unobstructed across the 48 final frames.

The 40 folding captures preserve the physical behavior at the new layer height. Desktop Eden 02 at 0.25 keeps the ground beneath the actors as the upright elements begin to fold; phone Eden 03 at 0.5 keeps the painted patch within the leaf and clear of the ribbon; and the 0.75/1 states retain the ground in the flattened printed composition. The Noah control sequences remain unchanged. Familiar edge-on darkening persists at middle fractions, but there is no new floor clipping, floating strip, gutter bleed, or loss of critical silhouettes. Sampled stills do not establish the smoothness of the transition.

Lifecycle evidence is coherent within its scope. Normal, delayed, and reduced-motion states show the floor; the missing-texture state falls back to the usable cream page; and the superseded request settles on Noah without a stale Eden layer. The supplied checks report all five scenarios passing. This supports graceful decoration ownership and recovery, not loading speed.

**Strongest remaining defect:** one identical lush-edged patch spans five different emotional beats, most noticeably softening the change in Eden 04–05. A future pass should author a restrained post-choice variant—drier center, fewer green edge accents, and the same transparent paper margins—rather than increasing detail everywhere or zooming closer.

Fixed five-point rubric: 1 absent/broken, 2 rudimentary, 3 coherent but limited, 4 polished with minor shortcomings, 5 directly demonstrated reference-level. Scores are review judgments, not user approval.
