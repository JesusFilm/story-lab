# Keep complete figures and complete loading scenes

The fixed full-spread capture rectangle clipped figures that extended beyond the paper when folded. The live upright scene was complete, so shrinking or replacing its artwork would solve the wrong problem.

The capture now measures the visible folded clone's current vertex bounds, including animated vertices and excluding hidden alternate meshes. It unions those bounds with the paper rectangle and fits one orthographic projection with a small margin at the paper's aspect ratio. The gutter remains at texture U=0.5, so both half-page samples retain one common scale and orientation. The upright scene is unchanged. Oversized folded compositions print slightly smaller than their literal hinge footprint. The live folded stage uses the same scale and Y offset at reveal, with Z compensated to keep its paper plane above the bookmark. During popup unfolding, the stage returns continuously to its original size and position. The upright composition is therefore unchanged. Flat popup layers use the same small Z offsets in live rendering and capture to prevent coplanar interference. Their self-shadows and support triangles remain hidden until unfolding begins, then their original shadow flags return. Visual review must judge this transition rather than accepting containment metadata alone.

Reduced motion previously avoided both offscreen print targets, but hid the old stage before its replacement was ready. It now moves the completed outgoing stage into a temporary holding group. Its geometry, maps and actor resources remain alive until the complete replacement is ready, navigation returns to the room, or the app is disposed. A superseded partial load keeps the same last-complete stage. This uses no print targets and no moving leaf. The holding stage is not interactive while replacement is pending; the interface continues to show its real loading status.

Actor-owned meshes are removed before disposing other page geometry, avoiding duplicate releases. Unit checks cover retaining original resources, independent partial construction, copied source identity, replacement and idempotent cleanup. Browser checks hold the family image request, inspect the retained source, then exercise completion, skip and room cancellation. The existing full-spread target limits and renderer-state restoration checks remain in place.

`npm run verify:scene` runs formatting, types, unit/content/audio/asset validation, build, the complete three-size journey/performance check, recovery, touch and all loading/race checks under the nested static URL. It explicitly skips the browser narration timing and continuity suites when those paths are unchanged; `npm run verify:all` remains the complete gate. Historical timing results must not be labeled as rerun by the scene command.
