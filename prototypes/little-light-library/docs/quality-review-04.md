# Independent review of quality round 04

Historical review: raw iteration captures and recordings were deleted during repository cleanup. The observations below record checks performed at the time; removed evidence is not available for reinspection. Current verification is in [review/latest](../review/latest/).

Inspected the supplied desktop/mobile Noah 05, 07 and 08 stills in `review/04-story-signs/`, compared them with round 03, and checked desktop Eden 03 for the earlier missing-subject concern. No new browser session, continuous-motion viewing or audio listening was performed. This is a focused visual checkpoint, not full acceptance or reference-parity verification.

**Retain this pass.** Noah 05 now has a clearly visible dove carrying foliage and a calmer water/land backdrop. Noah stands unobstructed beside the opening rather than behind its bottom rail. Both covenant spreads now have a large, unmistakable rainbow, including at 360px width. Family faces remain unobscured beneath the arch. These additions fix substantive gaps in the narrated story; they are not merely decoration. Reader text and controls remain intact at both sizes.

The two highest remaining issues are:

1. **The ark window still reads as a freestanding picture frame.** It is smaller and no longer blocks Noah, but there is no surrounding hull or wall to establish it as an opening in the ark. At mobile size, the dove is identifiable while the olive foliage becomes much less distinct. A modest timber-wall surround and a slightly clearer leaf silhouette would give the prop a narrative purpose and make the returning sign readable. Avoid restoring the large obstructive rectangle from round 03.
2. **The new rainbow's finish is visually disconnected from the illustrated actors/scenery.** It reads convincingly as an upright paper arch, which suits the book mechanism, but its perfectly uniform color bands and hard computer-drawn edges look like a diagram placed among painterly assets. Keep the legible silhouette and muted palette; add restrained paper/paint variation and more consistent edge treatment rather than replacing it with a blurry background rainbow. Its physical separation is a gain worth preserving.

The earlier Eden 03 missing-serpent observation remains visible in the checked desktop still: Adam, Eve and the tree are present, but the named serpent is not discernible. This was outside the three Noah spreads targeted by the current pass and remains an outstanding story-stage requirement, not something the new Noah assets resolve.
