# Independent quality review of round 23

Historical review: raw iteration captures and recordings were deleted during repository cleanup. The observations below record checks performed at the time; removed evidence is not available for reinspection. Current verification is in [review/latest](../review/latest/).

I inspected all 36 matched baseline/final stills in `before/` and `candidate-02/`: 28 normal/reduced acting states and eight half/full-fold controls across desktop and phone. I also inspected the four maximum-travel dove poses in `candidate-02-peaks/` and eight final-production normal, reduced, English, and Japanese activation stills in `creature-final/`. I did not inspect the implementation, launch a browser, play the raw recordings, or assess perceived continuous motion or sound.

**Retain candidate 02.** The baseline serpent and dove remain identical across sampled normal times, so their story roles read as scenery. Candidate 02 gives each a distinct action while keeping its support fixed. The serpent's head and upper neck lean toward Eve through t1–t3; desktop t2 is the strongest sampled presentation. Enlarged inspection shows a continuous coil-to-neck silhouette with no transparent tear, duplicate head, stretched branch, or displaced leaf. Phone t2 retains enough displacement to read as the serpent addressing Eve rather than the whole tree swaying.

Candidate 01 is rejected as the final dove treatment. Its 0.24-radian maximum changes the phone wing tip by only about six pixels and reads as the same raised-wing illustration when viewed alone. Candidate 02's 0.4-radian peak creates a clearly flatter flight silhouette. At desktop peak 0.525, both wings lower together while the head, body, tail, feet, olive sprig, and wooden frame stay fixed. Feather overlap covers both shoulder joins; there is no duplicated root or body hole. Phone peak 0.525 remains recognizable without comparison, and the wing tips stay inside the frame. The second sampled peak is equally coherent at both widths.

The broader compositions remain stable. Eve, Adam, fruit, Noah, text card, controls, ground, shadows, and page edges remain unobscured throughout the fixed normal states. Reduced-motion serpent and dove retain the original silhouettes. All eight half/full-fold controls likewise preserve the complete source artwork; differences from baseline are trace rendering noise rather than partition fragments. This establishes sampled geometry retention, not fold smoothness.

The final-production interaction evidence places concise labels at the creatures rather than over the reading card or neighboring actors. English serpent and dove remain locally associated on phone; Japanese 蛇 and 鳩 fit cleanly. Desktop labels remain attached to the intended painted subjects, and neither covers a neighboring human or the reader card. Reduced-motion serpent and dove activation preserve the static creatures while retaining visible feedback. The final gate records all eight production cases successfully, but stills do not establish touch latency, audio quality, or the feel of the motion.

**Strongest remaining defect:** the dove is small inside a dark freestanding frame, so its wingbeat is clearest near maximum travel and becomes subtle in the ordinary t4 sample, especially on phone. Increasing amplitude again would risk the left wing approaching the frame. Future improvement should use timing/hold or a small local frame response rather than enlarge the wing rotation further.

Fixed five-point rubric: 1 absent/broken, 2 rudimentary, 3 coherent but limited, 4 polished with minor shortcomings, 5 directly demonstrated reference-level. Scores are review judgments, not user approval.
