# Little Light garden floor

Original built-in ImageGen ground illustration for the horizontal Eden pages in Little Light Library. Muted grass, earth, small stones and flowers connect the upright paper actors with their garden setting. Follow the [current visual guide](../../../styles/little-light-library/README.md).

`garden-floor-source.png` preserves the original 1536×1024 RGBA output. [Exact prompt](prompt.txt). The prototype owns its independent 1024×683 WebP derivative at quality84, with generated alpha preserved. This is a painted material, not a new 3D model. No third-party source image was supplied; no attribution requirement was supplied by the generator. The image is invented garden staging, not archaeological or scriptural evidence.

The illustration spans two page-local surfaces with a visible central gutter and paper margins. Runtime integration and acceptance are recorded in the [round20 log](../../../prototypes/little-light-library/review/README.md).
