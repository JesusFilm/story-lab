# Shepherd story sequence

Eight native ImageGen illustrations extending the existing announcement and
Nativity references, generated 12 September 2026. Visual guide:
[Follow the Light](../../../styles/follow-the-light/README.md).

- `watch.png`: quiet watch before the announcement.
- `reassurance.png`: alarm gives way to attentive relief.
- `gathering.png`: more angels join the messenger.
- `host.png`: the heavenly host and joyful shepherds.
- `departure.png`: setting out for Bethlehem after the angels depart.
- `sharing.png`: telling villagers the news.
- `mary.png`: Mary pondering beside the manger.
- `return.png`: returning to the flock in praise.

Original PNGs and exact prompts are preserved. Continuity anchors were the
prototype's existing `announcement.jpg` and `nativity.jpg`. Shepherd Adventure owns
compressed JPEG runtime copies. The compositions, winged angels, architecture and
dawn are illustrative choices, not claims of historical precision.
