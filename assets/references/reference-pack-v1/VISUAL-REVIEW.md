Shared observations: the images consistently use semi-realistic rendered game materials and natural proportions, with blue/ivory nighttime scenes and warm limestone Jerusalem scenes. No obvious modern objects, logos or unintended UI were noticed. Clothing continuity is suitable for concepts, but faces and garment cuts are not exact registered model turnarounds. Temple ornament, animal species styling, angel appearance and costumes are artistic interpretations.

The requested star-beacon treatment merges a visual from Matthew's magi account with Luke's shepherd scene. The Jerusalem witness trail, compact district and condensed game duration are also adaptations. See the storyline notes for the relevant passages.
