# Stone nativity stall

An open-front stone shelter with enclosed side and back walls, a short left wing, timber door, reed-and-timber roof and hay-filled wall troughs. The generated rear includes a small auxiliary awning. The prototype fits the model to 5.5 × 4.5 m and lowers its thick earth base by 25 cm. This is illustrative architecture, not a verified historical reconstruction.
